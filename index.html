be

We use be to say what someone or something is. am, is, and are are shapes of be.

I am a teacher.

He is a doctor.

You are a pilot.

How to form

I am a teacher.
He is a doctor.
They are students.

More examples

She is a student.

It is a bag.

We are friends.

They are students.

Common problems

He a doctor.

He is a doctor.

The be word is missing.

I is a teacher.

I am a teacher.

I takes am.

They is students.

They are students.

They takes are.
