Lesson 1

be = am, are, is

We use be to say what someone or something is.

am, is, and are are shapes of be.

be changes shape

I→am
he / she / it→is
you / we / they→are

You means one or many.

Same word. Three shapes.

How to form

Three pieces that fit: who + be + what.

Iama teacher.
Heisa doctor.
Theyarestudents.

Examples

I am a teacher.

He is a doctor.

She is a student.

It is a bag.

You are a pilot.

We are friends.

They are students.

Do not do this

He a doctor.

He is a doctor.

I is a teacher.

I am a teacher.

They is students.

They are students.
