be = am, are, is

He is a doctor.

We use be to say what someone or something is. am, is, and are are shapes of be.

Who Shape
I → am
he / she / it → is
you / we / they → are

More examples

I am a teacher.

She is a student.

It is a bag.

You are a pilot.

We are friends.

They are students.

Common problems

He a doctor.

He is a doctor.

The be word is missing.

I is a teacher.

I am a teacher.

I takes am.

They are be students.

They are students.

Use are. Do not add be as well.
